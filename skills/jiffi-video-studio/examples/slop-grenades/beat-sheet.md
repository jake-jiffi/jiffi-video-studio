# Worked example: "Slop Grenades" (about 75s)

Angle: The joke is that AI turns one good sentence into a mess everyone
has to clean up, and the fix is reading it before you send it.
Metaphor: a cola bottle is your idea, AI-added "extras" are mints dropped in,
and throwing it over the partition is sending it without reading.
Source line: Shopify's CEO used "slop grenades" on The Knowledge Project
(Sept 2026). Show as an on-screen source line only. Never depict him.
